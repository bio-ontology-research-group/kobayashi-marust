# C1e: coverage of the certified fast SHIQ route

Stage C1e. Branch `v170/c1e-coverage` (from `development/v1.7.0`).
Scratch, scripts and raw results:
`/mnt/data1/leechuck/km-work/v170/scratch/c1e/`.

## 1. Diagnosis

Method. The probe gained `KM_SRIQF_DUMP=<path>` (the ontology the
generator sees, in the `--sriq` format, after `encTransQ`), a `reqs`
line in the `--sriq` format (request a chosen set of names, for single
roots), and `KM_SRIQF_DIAG=1` (nodes per context, parents per context,
seed sizes, the largest contexts with the names their nodes add, the
nodes with the largest pools, the contexts with several nodes and many
parents). The reductions below cut the dumped normal form to single
roots or to the axioms of one pattern.

### 1.1 Top-level disjunctions (10019, 6485, 6333, 11554, 1646, 7877, 9012; also 10140, 6952)

Source pattern. An equivalence `A ≡ C ⊓ (=k R.D) ⊓ …` (chemistry:
10019, 6485; 10140 and 6952 have the same shape with `k` up to 6) has the
direction `C ⊓ (=k R.D) ⊓ … ⊑ A`. The translation names the complex
conjunct with a fresh name `x` and the top disjunction

    ⊤ ⊑ x ⊔ W≤ ⊔ W≥,   W≤ ⊑ ≤(k-1) R.D,   W≥ ⊑ ≥(k+1) R.D,   C ⊓ x ⊓ … ⊑ A.

A universal or a negated existential on the left (11554, 1646: `∀R.¬N`)
gives `⊤ ⊑ x ⊔ W`, `W ⊑ ∃R.N`, `x ⊓ B ⊑ A`. In the dump of 10019, all 24
top disjunctions have this shape: the marker `x` occurs in the head of
its top disjunction and in the body of one gci, nowhere else.

Growth. Every closed type must pick a head of every top disjunction, so
`br {a}` has up to `3ᵏ` branches in every context, also in contexts that
can never fire the body gci (an oxygen atom, a hydrogen atom). On 10019
the first `branches` call of a root does not return; on 10140 (6 top
disjunctions, 108 000 root contexts) generation reached 12 million nodes.

Reduction. One top disjunction with its body gci and two contexts that
do not hold `C` already shows the product: each context gets one node
per head, and the nodes differ only in names that nothing reads.

### 1.2 Branching on the definers that remain (10019, 6485)

After the elimination of section 2.1 the disjunction moves into the gci
of `C`: `C ⊑ A ⊔ W≤ ⊔ W≥ ⊔ …` (one gci per defined class, up to 7
heads). Only types with `C` branch, but a carbon type branches on all of
them at once: `br {C}` has 91 875 branches on the dumps of 10019 and
6485 (`py/brcount.py`). Merging definers with equal clauses (the
translation makes one fresh definer per occurrence of `≤0 R.D`) cuts it
to 2 231, adding the implied gcis between definers of one role and filler
(`W≤0 ⊑ W≤1`) to 1 637. Generation with merged definers still exceeds
120 s: 145 000 nodes in 655 contexts, 101 000 merges (R5), 82 s in the
merge trigger. The design has no way to leave a disjunction undecided:
km keeps `⊤ → x ∨ W≤ ∨ W≥` as a clause and resolves on demand.

### 1.3 Back-variant growth (11629, 9855, 786; 14572, 7361, 9724)

These are GALEN variants (`00034`-`00038`, `fullgalen`): 36 000 to
39 000 names, 13 466 lower clauses (most from existentials), 59
functional roles, 40 transitive roles, 49 inverse pairs.

- A single root is already large: requesting one name of 11629
  (`reqs 5`) gives 43 000 to 52 000 nodes in 60 s, for every root tried
  (5, 500, 1500, 3000), and the queue still grows.
- The type of a root grows by back propagation in steps: in the root
  context of name 684 the node types go 32 → 224 → 226 names; each step
  is a new node with its own pool and therefore its own child contexts,
  because the child seed holds `fwd t L` and `fwd` grows with the type.
- Child seeds are large (16 to 128 names, mostly universal fillers from
  inverse existentials on the left and the transitivity markers of
  `encTransQ`), and 17 409 of 19 967 contexts have distinct (seed,
  label) pairs: the projection `π` does not split them, the seeds do.
- The merge trigger dominates the time: after 1 200 s, 337 000 nodes,
  5.4 million specs, 115 000 merges, 885 s in the trigger, the queue
  still at 527 000 events. Pools hold 20 to 30 specs per live node;
  functional roles merge every pair of possibly counted specs that
  contain the definite union `M`.

The static back sets of section 3.3 remove the intermediate nodes of a
back-propagation chain but not the growth (26 000 nodes for one root in
60 s, half of them superseded).

### 1.4 Context explosion (6134; 15203 outside the 32)

6134 (FMA gross anatomy fragment): 1 285 existentials over two inverse
roles (`part_of` 25, `has_part` 16), 879 universals, 323 transitivity
markers. A single root gives over 20 000 nodes in 9 500 contexts for 42
of 74 sampled roots (`d0/6134roots.tsv`); 29 roots stay at 1 node.

The contexts carry the markers of the `has_part` chain from the root:
`∃part_of.X ⊑ Y` becomes `X ⊑ ∀has_part.Y` with a transitivity marker
`f ⊑ ∀has_part.f`, so every node below `X` along `has_part` holds `f`.
The seeds of the child contexts are (filler, markers of the path): 23 047
contexts with 23 047 distinct seeds for one root with the narrow `Rel`,
23 260 contexts and 21 522 distinct (seed, label) pairs with the full
`Rel`. So the full `Rel` (section 4 of `C1C-PROOF.md`) is not the cause;
the number of distinct marker sets along the paths is.

In the root contexts, R4 makes a variant for every candidate back set
when the forced set is already in the type; the children are branches of
small disjunctions with incomparable back sets, so variants of variants
give their unions (24 nodes in a root context, `extra` sets
`{529, 3525, 3526}`, `{3587, 3588}`, `{549, 708, 716, 3605, 3606}` and
their combinations).

### 1.5 Throughput (10140, 6952 after the elimination; 6333; 9012)

- 10140 and 6952 after the elimination: 108 000 contexts with one node
  each, pools of at most 42 specs; generation reaches 112 000 nodes in
  6 s and then crawls (1 000 events per 10 s) through 58 000 back-rule
  calls of 0.6 ms, each of which recomputes `branches` for a few new
  nodes; it runs out of memory at 16 GB after 150 s. Some contexts have
  16 000 parents, and every new node in such a context woke all of them.
- 6333: generation 31 s (56 587 nodes), `closedFastB` 176 s.
- 9012: generation 3 s (11 440 nodes), the certified elimination 70 s
  (the depth-first support search has no memo, the generator's has).

## 2. Strategies and their effect

Each strategy below is either a proved transformation or a proved
change of the check (section 3), or a generator change (validated
only). The route now runs `translateSRIQ`, `isSHIQFastB`, `encTransQ`,
`elimChecked`, `mergeChecked`, the generator, `closedFastB`, the
worklist elimination and the readout.

### 2.1 Elimination of body markers (proved; family 1.1)

`SRIQ/ElimMarkers.lean`. A fresh name `x` (index at least the number of
requested names) that occurs in the head of one gci `B ⊑ H` only (every
gci with `x` in the head is a copy of it), not in `B`, in no clause other
than gci bodies, and whose defining gci mentions no other eliminated
name, is removed by resolution: every gci `B' ⊑ H'` with `x ∈ B'` becomes
`(B' ∖ {x}) ∪ B ⊑ H' ∪ (H ∖ {x})`, and the defining gci goes away. The
top disjunction then becomes a disjunction in the gci of the body: only
types that hold the body branch. The hint (`elimHint`) is unproved and
checked (`hintOK`); when the check fails the ontology is used unchanged.

Effect: 11554 and 1646 go from timeout to 0.3 s and 0.4 s (36 and 37
names eliminated, 563 and 578 nodes); 9012 from timeout to 75 s
(61 names; 8 s with section 2.6); 6333 from timeout to generation in
31 s (82 names; the check then took 176 s, 48 s after section 2.4);
10140 and 6952 lose their top disjunctions (30 340 names) and reach the
back-propagation problem of section 1.5; 7877 (215 names) and 10019,
6485 (43 names) still time out (section 1.2).

### 2.2 Merging fresh names with equal universal clauses (proved; 10140, 6952)

`SRIQ/MergeNames.lean`. A fresh name whose positive occurrences are
universal fillers only (`a ⊑ ∀S.x`; other occurrences in gci bodies, on
the left of clauses, as the filler of at-most clauses) is fixed from
below by its universal clauses. Two such names with the same universal
clauses are identified (`renO`). On 10140, 19 158 copies of the name for
`∃R₉.C` (one per equivalence `Aᵢ ≡ Bᵢ ⊓ ∃R₉.C`) become one, so the node
with `C` pushes one name to its parent instead of 19 158; 59 756 names
merge in all. Generation of 10140 goes from out of memory at 150 s to
141 250 nodes in 214 s, and to 4.8 s with section 2.5.

### 2.3 Generator: batched parent wake-up, settled back plans (unproved)

A new node in a context with many parents (up to 16 000 on 10140) woke
every parent's back rule and merge trigger. The generator now batches
the wake-up per context, wakes a parent's back rule only when its last
plan was not settled (every need had a candidate and the forced set was
in the type) or the new node pushes a name the parent lacks, and its
merge trigger only when the context's union of types grew. No effect on
the answers (the closure is the same); it does not by itself fix 10140,
where `branches` was the cost (section 2.5).

### 2.4 Branch-cover hint index (proved; 6333 and all large inputs)

`brCovA` (does every branch of `s` contain a node of context `k`
containing `s`?) scanned all members of `k` for every call. The candidate
set now carries a hint `idx : (context, type) ↦ (node, position)`; the
check looks up each branch under its own sorted type, verifies the
position in the member list, the context, `s ⊆ type ⊆ branch`, and falls
back to the scan when the lookup fails. `brCovA_sound` is re-proved with
the lookup. 6333: check 176 s → 48 s.

### 2.5 Hub index for gcis in many bodies (proved in the check; 10140, 6952)

After the merge of section 2.2, one name is in the bodies of 38 316 gcis;
both the generator's `branches` and the check's `brA`/`closedIA` scanned
them every time that name joined a set. The index `mkIx` now holds, for a
name in at least 64 gci bodies (a hub), the gcis whose body holds only
the hub, and by every other body name the gcis with both (`hubOne`,
`hubBy`). When a hub joins a set, `trigL` returns only the gcis whose
other body names are in the set (listed by an `acc` argument that
`brAux` and `brAuxA` carry identically). `br_guided` holds for any
queue, so `BranchCov` keeps its meaning; `mem_trigL` proves that the
closedness check `closedIA` still sees every gci whose body holds.
10140: generation 214 s → 4.8 s, check 28 s, total 40 s.

### 2.6 Support search with a memo (proved; 9012)

The certified support test `dfsG` had no memo; the generator's has.
`dfsM` keeps the states (remaining needs and limits) found to have no
solution and is proved to decide the same problem when the fuel exceeds
the needs (`dfsM_iff`, used in `suppF_iff`). 9012: elimination 70 s →
2.4 s.

### 2.7 Tried without success

- **Static back sets** (generator option `KM_SRIQF_STATICBACK=1`, off):
  a node whose type misses the names that the witness of one of its
  lower clauses must push back (computed from the ontology and the Horn
  closure of the witness seed) is superseded by the variant with them
  and gets no pool. Sound as a closure exemption (a type-maximal
  simulating node always holds these names), not proved. On one GALEN
  root (11629, name 5) it halves the contexts (9 491 instead of
  17 939 after 60 s) but the growth continues (26 000 nodes, half of
  them superseded).
- **Definer merging and implied gcis for 10019 and 6485** (measured on
  dumps with a Python rewrite, `py/merge.py`): merging the definers with
  equal clauses cuts `br {C}` from 91 875 to 2 231 branches, adding
  `W≤0 ⊑ W≤1` to 1 637, but generation still exceeds 300 s (6485:
  263 000 nodes, 154 000 merges, 220 s in the merge trigger). Not
  implemented in Lean.
- **Disabling pool inheritance** on 11629: no change in growth.
- **Lazy definers (R7)** as a closure condition: a design note only
  (section 6); the readout conclusion `∀ b, b ∈ L ↔ EntailsSub` covers
  the definer names too, and a lazily chosen definer in a root type
  breaks it.

## 3. New and changed theorem statements

All proved without `sorry`, `axiom`, `native_decide`, `implemented_by` or
`extern`; `#print axioms` shows at most `[propext, Classical.choice,
Quot.sound]` for every statement below (logs under
`scratch/c1e/log-*.txt`).

New (`SRIQ/ElimMarkers.lean`, `SRIQ/MergeNames.lean`):

```lean
theorem Elim.entailsSub_elimChecked {a b : Fin n} (ha : a.val < bound) (hb : b.val < bound) :
    EntailsSub (elimChecked O bound) a b ↔ EntailsSub O a b
theorem Elim.isALCHIQ_elimChecked (h : IsALCHIQ O) : IsALCHIQ (elimChecked O bound)
theorem Merge.entailsSub_mergeChecked {a b : Fin n} (ha : a.val < bound) (hb : b.val < bound) :
    EntailsSub (mergeChecked O bound) a b ↔ EntailsSub O a b
theorem Merge.isALCHIQ_mergeChecked (h : IsALCHIQ O) : IsALCHIQ (mergeChecked O bound)
```

with the model maps `models_elimO`/`models_ext` (eliminated names hold
where the body of their defining gci holds and no other head name does)
and `models_renO_meet`/`models_of_renO` (a merged name holds where every
name of its class holds).

New in the check (`SRIQ/FastCheckAProof.lean`, `SRIQ/FastElimAProof.lean`):

```lean
theorem mem_trigL {y : Fin n} {g : Nat} (hg : g ∈ getL (mkIx O).gciBy y.val) {acc : List Nat}
    (hacc : ∀ b ∈ (gciAt (mkIx O) g).1, b ≠ y → b.val ∈ acc) : g ∈ trigL (mkIx O) y acc
theorem dfsM_iff [DecidableEq α] {needs lims : List (Nat × Nat)} :
    (dfsM cnt cs ((needs.map (·.2)).sum + 1) needs lims ∅).1 = true ↔ HasSol cnt cs needs lims
```

Changed definitions with the same theorem statements: `brAux`/`br` and
`brAuxA`/`brA` take the `acc` list and queue through `trigL`
(`brAux_guided`, `br_guided`, `brAuxA_cover`, `brCovA_sound` re-proved);
`closedIA` uses `trigL` (`closedIA_sound` now stated for `mkIx O`);
`brCovA` consults `CandA.idx` (`brCovA_sound` re-proved); `suppF` uses
`dfsM` (`suppF_iff` re-proved).

Unchanged statements: `fastClassifyA_spec` (same hypotheses and
conclusion) and `owlSHIQFast_sound` (same conclusion). The route
`owlSHIQFast` now classifies
`mergeChecked (elimChecked (encTransQ T.ontology).2 T.named.length) T.named.length`;
its proof rewrites the requested bits with the two `entailsSub_*`
lemmas (the requested names are the first `T.named.length` indices).
