# C1e: coverage of the certified fast SHIQ route

Stage C1e. Branch `v170/c1e-coverage` (from `development/v1.7.0`).
Scratch, scripts and raw results:
`/mnt/data1/leechuck/km-work/v170/scratch/c1e/`.

## 1. Diagnosis

Method. The probe gained `KM_SRIQF_DUMP=<path>` (the ontology the
generator sees, in the `--sriq` format, after `encTransQ`), a `reqs`
line in the `--sriq` format (request a chosen set of names, for single
roots), and `KM_SRIQF_DIAG=1` (nodes per context, parents per context,
seed sizes, the largest contexts with the names their nodes add, the
nodes with the largest pools, the contexts with several nodes and many
parents). The reductions below cut the dumped normal form to single
roots or to the axioms of one pattern.

### 1.1 Top-level disjunctions (10019, 6485, 6333, 11554, 1646, 7877, 9012; also 10140, 6952)

Source pattern. An equivalence `A ≡ C ⊓ (=k R.D) ⊓ …` (chemistry:
10019, 6485; 10140 and 6952 have the same shape with `k` up to 6) has the
direction `C ⊓ (=k R.D) ⊓ … ⊑ A`. The translation names the complex
conjunct with a fresh name `x` and the top disjunction

    ⊤ ⊑ x ⊔ W≤ ⊔ W≥,   W≤ ⊑ ≤(k-1) R.D,   W≥ ⊑ ≥(k+1) R.D,   C ⊓ x ⊓ … ⊑ A.

A universal or a negated existential on the left (11554, 1646: `∀R.¬N`)
gives `⊤ ⊑ x ⊔ W`, `W ⊑ ∃R.N`, `x ⊓ B ⊑ A`. In the dump of 10019, all 24
top disjunctions have this shape: the marker `x` occurs in the head of
its top disjunction and in the body of one gci, nowhere else.

Growth. Every closed type must pick a head of every top disjunction, so
`br {a}` has up to `3ᵏ` branches in every context, also in contexts that
can never fire the body gci (an oxygen atom, a hydrogen atom). On 10019
the first `branches` call of a root does not return; on 10140 (6 top
disjunctions, 108 000 root contexts) generation reached 12 million nodes.

Reduction. One top disjunction with its body gci and two contexts that
do not hold `C` already shows the product: each context gets one node
per head, and the nodes differ only in names that nothing reads.

### 1.2 Branching on the definers that remain (10019, 6485)

After the elimination of section 2.1 the disjunction moves into the gci
of `C`: `C ⊑ A ⊔ W≤ ⊔ W≥ ⊔ …` (one gci per defined class, up to 7
heads). Only types with `C` branch, but a carbon type branches on all of
them at once: `br {C}` has 91 875 branches on the dumps of 10019 and
6485 (`py/brcount.py`). Merging definers with equal clauses (the
translation makes one fresh definer per occurrence of `≤0 R.D`) cuts it
to 2 231, adding the implied gcis between definers of one role and filler
(`W≤0 ⊑ W≤1`) to 1 637. Generation with merged definers still exceeds
120 s: 145 000 nodes in 655 contexts, 101 000 merges (R5), 82 s in the
merge trigger. The design has no way to leave a disjunction undecided:
km keeps `⊤ → x ∨ W≤ ∨ W≥` as a clause and resolves on demand.

### 1.3 Back-variant growth (11629, 9855, 786; 14572, 7361, 9724)

These are GALEN variants (`00034`-`00038`, `fullgalen`): 36 000 to
39 000 names, 13 466 lower clauses (most from existentials), 59
functional roles, 40 transitive roles, 49 inverse pairs.

- A single root is already large: requesting one name of 11629
  (`reqs 5`) gives 43 000 to 52 000 nodes in 60 s, for every root tried
  (5, 500, 1500, 3000), and the queue still grows.
- The type of a root grows by back propagation in steps: in the root
  context of name 684 the node types go 32 → 224 → 226 names; each step
  is a new node with its own pool and therefore its own child contexts,
  because the child seed holds `fwd t L` and `fwd` grows with the type.
- Child seeds are large (16 to 128 names, mostly universal fillers from
  inverse existentials on the left and the transitivity markers of
  `encTransQ`), and 17 409 of 19 967 contexts have distinct (seed,
  label) pairs: the projection `π` does not split them, the seeds do.
- The merge trigger dominates the time: after 1 200 s, 337 000 nodes,
  5.4 million specs, 115 000 merges, 885 s in the trigger, the queue
  still at 527 000 events. Pools hold 20 to 30 specs per live node;
  functional roles merge every pair of possibly counted specs that
  contain the definite union `M`.

The static back sets of section 3.3 remove the intermediate nodes of a
back-propagation chain but not the growth (26 000 nodes for one root in
60 s, half of them superseded).

### 1.4 Context explosion (6134; 15203 outside the 32)

6134 (FMA gross anatomy fragment): 1 285 existentials over two inverse
roles (`part_of` 25, `has_part` 16), 879 universals, 323 transitivity
markers. A single root gives over 20 000 nodes in 9 500 contexts for 42
of 74 sampled roots (`d0/6134roots.tsv`); 29 roots stay at 1 node.

The contexts carry the markers of the `has_part` chain from the root:
`∃part_of.X ⊑ Y` becomes `X ⊑ ∀has_part.Y` with a transitivity marker
`f ⊑ ∀has_part.f`, so every node below `X` along `has_part` holds `f`.
The seeds of the child contexts are (filler, markers of the path): 23 047
contexts with 23 047 distinct seeds for one root with the narrow `Rel`,
23 260 contexts and 21 522 distinct (seed, label) pairs with the full
`Rel`. So the full `Rel` (section 4 of `C1C-PROOF.md`) is not the cause;
the number of distinct marker sets along the paths is.

In the root contexts, R4 makes a variant for every candidate back set
when the forced set is already in the type; the children are branches of
small disjunctions with incomparable back sets, so variants of variants
give their unions (24 nodes in a root context, `extra` sets
`{529, 3525, 3526}`, `{3587, 3588}`, `{549, 708, 716, 3605, 3606}` and
their combinations).

### 1.5 Throughput (10140, 6952 after the elimination; 6333; 9012)

- 10140 and 6952 after the elimination: 108 000 contexts with one node
  each, pools of at most 42 specs; generation reaches 112 000 nodes in
  6 s and then crawls (1 000 events per 10 s) through 58 000 back-rule
  calls of 0.6 ms, each of which recomputes `branches` for a few new
  nodes; it runs out of memory at 16 GB after 150 s. Some contexts have
  16 000 parents, and every new node in such a context woke all of them.
- 6333: generation 31 s (56 587 nodes), `closedFastB` 176 s.
- 9012: generation 3 s (11 440 nodes), the certified elimination 70 s
  (the depth-first support search has no memo, the generator's has).
