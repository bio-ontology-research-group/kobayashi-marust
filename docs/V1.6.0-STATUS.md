## 2026-11-09, late evening: survey and the phase 2 decision

The survey `docs/history/SURVEY-HT-CB-COMPLETENESS-2026-11-09.md` (read-only, Lean sources and docs)
found: the hypertableau totality theorems need a `CheckedRegularControlProducer`
that nothing constructs executably (an always-rejecting or always-frontier
producer is not admissible, so the producer's existence at large budgets is
the decision itself); every CB completeness theorem is conditional on
supplied terminal data, `CBSourceEqualityCanonicalModel` stops before its
final satisfaction theorem, no executable CB saturation exists and
`Termination.lean` bounds core counts only; the rule route has checker
soundness only. By contrast `CompletenessStrategy.saturate_decides` is a
finished, hypothesis-free exact decision for the ALC clause language, and
`CBALCEncoding` ties that language to the common source.

Decision (plan section 5): phase 2 is an ALC reference procedure by
computable type elimination, not a hypertableau producer. Brief
`v160-alc-reference.md`; agent `v160-alc-reference` (agy, Gemini 4 Argon),
worktree `.work/worktrees/v160-alc-reference`, branch `v160/alc-reference`.
The HT producer stays the post-v1.6.0 route to role hierarchies, inverses
and chains; CB remains phase 3 at research scale.

Build note: Lake 5.0 here has no `-j`; use `LEAN_NUM_THREADS=n`. `universe`
is a Lean keyword, hence `contextUniverse`.

  ABox-inconsistency fast path, the finite-rule projection and two
  candidate paths unguarded; the stage spawns the binary with
  `std::process::Command` rather than `CheckerCommand`; the published line

shipped stage must publish on the recognised fragment within the cap or
the release claim is hollow. Two follow-up agents: `v160-reference-speed`
