- 2026-10-28, second: nominal-mode answers come only from the complete

  incomplete Sequoia ordering, are not coverage losses.

## Decisions taken

- Exact regime in nominal mode (the Sequoia ordering is incomplete with the
  nominal ground context; 12-clause minimal case and generated seed 297).
  Cost: the bug-1 reproducer goes from 0.17 s to about 100 s per nominal
  route; the hub + W2/W3 variant gives no answer within 900 s; 9800 and
  10874 time out; total time on 201 gated ORE files 4811 → 5437 s. The
  dead-disjunct encodings did not help (section 7).
- The five merge-and-count data-assertion inputs stay with honest DROPPED;
  native data-value reasoning is v1.5.5 work.

## Open defects (not fixed in v1.5.4)

- Per-context ordering (Sequoia outside the nominal ground context): the way
  back to speed; needs a completeness argument and Lean (v1.6.0 roadmap).
- Forced non-nominal routes ignore ABox inconsistency and approximate
  nominals (default route wrong on the `≤2 u` reproducer). Automatic routing
  never selects them for such inputs.
- Generated-seed timeouts on the nominal routes (about 60 of 400); the sibling
  at-most-one prototype removes 14 and adds 3; parked.
- Bridge datatype-extension unit test (`bridge_tbox_datatype_extension_literal_is_head_only`)
  declines after the `rdfs:Literal` folding; to be reassigned to pi-opencode.
- Pre-existing `orchestrate::` test failures (2) and the `cb_live_state`
  Lean-checker test that needs the external checker.
- The clause path of the bridge (`KM_NO_SOURCE_TBOX=1`) is incomplete on 235
  of 700 seeds; automatic routing does not use it for inputs with class axioms.
