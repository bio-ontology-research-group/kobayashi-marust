## Goal and contract

v1.5.4 replaces the v1.5.3 route selector, which was fitted to known ORE
inputs, with a general policy (`KM_ROUTING_POLICY=general`, default). The
acceptance contract is `docs/V1.5.4-ACCEPTANCE.md`. It has three approved
amendments:

- 2026-10-27: proved source certificates are exempt from the admission floor.
- 2026-10-28: inputs v1.5.3 answered by silently discarding data-property
  assertions are not coverage losses (10425, 15655, 4410, 4572, 5368).
- 2026-10-28, second: nominal-mode answers come only from the complete
  (unordered) regime; 9800 and 10874, which v1.5.3 answered under the
  incomplete Sequoia ordering, are not coverage losses.

## Decisions taken

- Nominal mode uses the context regime of the Sequoia ordering where the
  router selects the ordering (2026-10-29, replacing the 2026-10-28 decision
  to stay unordered). Grounds: the plain ordering is incomplete with the
  nominal ground context (12-clause case, seed 297); the unordered regime is
  incomplete too (`p1_cross_individual`, five axioms, HermiT `Q ⊑ B`); the
  context regime answers both, agrees with HermiT on 2,433 differential runs
  with no regime-caused wrong answer, leaves the ORE taxonomy unchanged and
  recovers 10874 (6 s) and 15655 (5 s). The ground-level exchange theorem is
  proved in Lean; the mapping from KM's announcement guard to the theorem is
  argued in `docs/PER-CONTEXT-ORDERING.md` section 6, not mechanised. The CB
  certificate checker still models the unordered regime, so runs with
  `KM_CB_LEAN_REQUIRED` keep it.
- Forced routes fail closed: a forced route never publishes an approximate
  answer without `KM_ALLOW_APPROXIMATE=1`.
- The five merge-and-count data-assertion inputs stay with honest DROPPED;
  native data-value reasoning is v1.5.5 work.

## Release decision (open, 2026-10-30 morning)

Everything in the goal except G8's mean bound is done: no known soundness
or completeness defect remains, the Lean gates and both test suites pass
from the tag, and the held-out half lost nothing. On the 556 held-out
inputs verified in all four runs the v1.5.4 protocol run sums to 2,561.8 s
against 2,186.5 s for the same-day v1.5.3 run (ratio 1.172; the bound
allows 2,405.1 s, so the excess is 156.7 s). The excess has three parts:

| Part | Inputs | Excess | Nature |
|---|---|---:|---|
| checked subset blocking, card arm | 205 | 153 s | soundness fix (published `CONSISTENT` on an inconsistent ontology) |
| data assertions carried into the rules-consistency worker | 7278, 3726 | 48 s | soundness fix (silently discarded assertions) |
| general policy sends former bridge inputs to `certified_nominals` | 1673, 1938, 11005, 10754, 5289, 15226 | 166 s | policy choice inside the contract (certified route over the Konclude bridge) |

The rest of the 556 inputs net to about -210 s in v1.5.4's favour, with
run-to-run noise of 6 percent on the mean (two runs of the same binary and
policy). 7499 shows the same blocking cost on the design half.

Options:

1. Release the tag as it stands with a G8 amendment: the mean bound is
   relaxed for the measured cost of the two soundness fixes, or the
   comparison excludes the inputs whose v1.5.3 answer rested on the fixed
   defects. Median, coverage, wrong-answer and gate criteria are met. The
   blocking fix ships in v1.5.5 after a longer soak. Release assets for
   `80c8f82a` are prepared under `.work/v154-release-assets-80c8f82a/`.
2. Re-tag with the blocking fix (8565b673): new tag, the four gates, Rust
   and Java from it (about two hours), a PGO build with the glibc wrapper,
   and a second protocol run on the held-out half (the maintainer must
   accept that the half is used twice). The projected mean is 1.11 to 1.12,
   so the re-run would land near the bound on either side and an amendment
   would probably still be needed. The change is 376 lines of new
   incremental scan in the native worker, validated by a per-pass
   comparison with the full scan on two million passes and the 700-seed
   differential, reviewed once.
3. Hold v1.5.4 until v1.5.5 blocking and data-value work brings the mean
   inside the bound without an amendment.

Recommendation: option 1. The mean miss is the price of two soundness
fixes and a certification choice, each documented, and the fix on the
branch does not by itself bring the mean inside the bound. The contract is
the maintainer's, so the amendment is not written until it is approved.
