## Goal and contract

v1.5.4 replaces the v1.5.3 route selector, which was fitted to known ORE
inputs, with a general policy (`KM_ROUTING_POLICY=general`, default). The
acceptance contract is `docs/V1.5.4-ACCEPTANCE.md`. It has three approved
amendments:

- 2026-10-27: proved source certificates are exempt from the admission floor.
- 2026-10-28: inputs v1.5.3 answered by silently discarding data-property
  assertions are not coverage losses (10425, 15655, 4410, 4572, 5368).
- 2026-10-28, second: nominal-mode answers come only from the complete
  (unordered) regime; 9800 and 10874, which v1.5.3 answered under the
  incomplete Sequoia ordering, are not coverage losses.

## Decisions taken

- Nominal mode uses the context regime of the Sequoia ordering where the
  router selects the ordering (2026-10-29, replacing the 2026-10-28 decision
  to stay unordered). Grounds: the plain ordering is incomplete with the
  nominal ground context (12-clause case, seed 297); the unordered regime is
  incomplete too (`p1_cross_individual`, five axioms, HermiT `Q ⊑ B`); the
  context regime answers both, agrees with HermiT on 2,433 differential runs
  with no regime-caused wrong answer, leaves the ORE taxonomy unchanged and
  recovers 10874 (6 s) and 15655 (5 s). The ground-level exchange theorem is
  proved in Lean; the mapping from KM's announcement guard to the theorem is
  argued in `docs/PER-CONTEXT-ORDERING.md` section 6, not mechanised. The CB
  certificate checker still models the unordered regime, so runs with
  `KM_CB_LEAN_REQUIRED` keep it.
- Forced routes fail closed: a forced route never publishes an approximate
  answer without `KM_ALLOW_APPROXIMATE=1`.
- The five merge-and-count data-assertion inputs stay with honest DROPPED;
  native data-value reasoning is v1.5.5 work.

## Open defects

No open soundness or completeness defect is known on any route. Every item
listed in the afternoon edition of this file is fixed and merged.

Not release blockers (v1.5.5 per the amendments):

- Generated-seed timeouts on the nominal routes (about 60 of 400); nine generated files answer only under the unordered regime within 60 s (R2 cost).
- Data values above the 512-literal cap and the five merge-and-count inputs (honest DROPPED); QUDT (issue #4) needs native data-value reasoning.
- Disjunctive ground equalities across an unannounced individual are not covered by R3 (no counterexample known).
- Bridge clause translation (`KM_NO_SOURCE_TBOX=1`, diagnostic only) misses `≤2` when a predecessor must be counted, through the completion's reuse of saturated nodes (ignored test `ht_bridge_clause_translation_known_misses`).
- Binary object-atom DL-safe rules beside a TBox nominal and a data property get no verdict on the automatic route (the rule checker would need the sorted stream).
- Checked subset blocking: 7499 goes from 25 s to 98 s on auto; forced `ht_general` on inverse-role inputs blocks rarely and times out more; the bridge defers on 7 generated inputs it used to answer in 0.03 s.
- `data_assertions_carried::unproxied_assertions_can_hide_a_clash` runs 61–64 s against a 60 s bound under load.
- Pre-existing `orchestrate::` test failures (2) and 20 `tableau::` lib tests that need the gate-only Lean checkers (the CI recipe skips them).
