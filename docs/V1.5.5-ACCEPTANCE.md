# v1.5.5 acceptance contract: correctness, speed, Lean

Status: set by the maintainer on 2026-10-30 as the goal of the v1.5.5
campaign. No amendment exists. A performance amendment is not allowed for
this release.

## Goal

Deliver KM v1.5.5 from the two v1.5.5 branches merged into
`development/v1.5.5` (`fix/v155-subset-blocking-cost`,
`fix/v155-rules-worker-cost`) plus any further fixes needed, so that the
release is correct, as fast as v1.5.3 within the v1.5.4 bound, and covered by
Lean where its calculi or encodings changed.

## Requirements

### C1. Correctness

- Every known soundness and completeness defect is fixed. The list at the
  start of the campaign is the "Open defects" section of
  `docs/V1.5.4-STATUS.md` (2026-10-30 edition) plus the fr282 dropped-rule
  miss found on 2026-10-30 (`results/agents/2026-10-30/rules-worker-cost/`).
- The nominal-mode items deferred from v1.5.4 are fixed: the generated-seed
  timeouts on the nominal routes, ORE 9800 and 10874 under the complete
  regime, the DL-safe rule inputs without a verdict, and the R3 question
  about disjunctive ground equalities across an unannounced individual
  (proved or fixed).
- Data values: no answer depends on the 512-literal cap; the merge-and-count
  inputs and QUDT are answered without `DROPPED`.
- No wrong answer on the full regression pool and on the 2026-10-29 and
  2026-10-30 differential seeds (HermiT gold where the seeds have one).
- Answers are exact everywhere. An input KM cannot answer exactly is refused
  with a reason; it is never answered approximately or with silently
  dropped axioms.

### S1. Speed

A PGO build of the release candidate meets the original G8 bound of
`docs/V1.5.4-ACCEPTANCE.md`, compared with v1.5.3 on the held-out half, on
the same Ibex node type, under the v1.5.3 protocol (one core, 240 s, 20 GiB,
PGO build, scoped watchdog):

- verified inputs ≥ v1.5.3 (no loss);
- mean and median time on common inputs ≤ v1.5.3 × 1.10;
- more verified inputs than Konclude on a same-day Konclude run. The mean
  and median against Konclude on common inputs are measured and reported
  (the original G8 wording also asks KM to be faster in both); they do not
  gate the release, because on the held-out half the v1.5.3 baseline itself
  is slower than Konclude in mean (same-day v1.5.3 run of 2026-11-01:
  4.03 s against the 2026-10-04 Konclude run's 2.59 s on 535 common inputs,
  median 0.235 s against 0.241 s), so that clause cannot tell a regression
  from the baseline. This follows the goal statement of 2026-10-30, which
  names "more verified than Konclude on a same-day run" as the Konclude part
  of the bound. It is an interpretation recorded for the maintainer, not a
  performance amendment: the v1.5.3 × 1.10 bound and no-loss stand.

### L1. Lean

- Every calculus or encoding change in v1.5.5 is covered by Lean proofs or
  by a Lean checker whose acceptance implies the change is exact: the
  incremental checked subset blocking, the inert-sort release in the rule
  checker, and anything else the campaign touches.
- The four Lean gates pass from the exact tag.
- The checkers-on suite planned for v1.5.5 in
  `docs/CERTIFICATION-ROADMAP.md` is in place and its results are recorded.

### R1. Release

v1.5.5 is released only when C1, S1 and L1 hold, with the full Rust, Java and
Lean gates run from the tag, and with this document updated to record the
evidence for each requirement. No performance amendment is added.

**Finding on the Konclude count of record.** `docs/RELEASE-1.5.4.md` and
v1.5.4's `build-completion.json` state that v1.5.4 verifies more held-out
inputs than Konclude (558 against 554, Konclude run of 2026-10-04). The 554

it counted a Konclude answer as solved only when the v1.5.3 record's
comparison status was the literal `agreement`, so the 22 inputs whose status

answer) were discarded along with the two taxonomy disagreements. Counted
fairly, Konclude solved 576 of the 585 held-out inputs on 2026-10-04 (578
answered) and 575 on 2026-11-01. No KM release has verified more held-out
inputs than Konclude under this protocol: v1.5.3 560, v1.5.4 558, this
candidate 562. The clause therefore cannot be met by a speed recovery; it
needs 14 or more of the 16 inputs above to be answered within 240 s and
20 GiB, which no v1.5.x line has done.
