# KM v1.6.0 acceptance

v1.6.0 is the completeness release. Its theorem and plan are in
`docs/V1.6.0-COMPLETENESS-PLAN.md`. The release is accepted when every
clause below holds on the tagged commit, with the evidence recorded here.

## P1. The completeness capstone is a theorem

`lean/ContextCalculus/KMSupervisorInstance.lean` builds, and the routing
gate's `#print axioms` log reports for each of

- `KMSupervisorInstance.kmSupervisor_sound`
- `KMSupervisorInstance.kmSupervisor_complete_on_EL`
- `KMSupervisorInstance.kmSupervisor_sound_and_complete_on_EL`
- `KMConcreteWorkerAdapters.elReferenceExecution_completeAt`
- `KMConcreteWorkerAdapters.elReferenceAccept_sound`
- `ELReference.referenceBit_exact`
- `ELCommonSourceRecognizer.recognizeEL_entails_iff`

at most `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`; no `sorryAx`, no
`Lean.ofReduceBool`. No completeness field remains in
`CertifiedSupervisor` or `ConcreteSupervisor`; coverage is the hypothesis
`CoversAt`, discharged for the EL fragment by the reference procedure.

## P2. The fragment is not vacuous

`ELCommonSourceRecognizer.recognizeEL` accepts the common clause source of
every EL input of the checkers-on suite that the Rust frontend classifies
as EL (the `elc` route of `results/agents/2026-10-30/checkers-on-suite/`,
593 inputs), measured by running `km-el-reference --recognize-only` on the
normalised clause stream of each. The number accepted and the reasons for
every rejection are recorded in `docs/V1.6.0-STATUS.md`.

## P3. The shipped system runs the reference stage

`km classify` on the automatic route runs the Lean reference stage after
the selected route and its exact fallback have declined
(`KM_REFERENCE_STAGE=0` disables it). Rust tests force every production
route to decline on EL and ALC fixtures and check that the published
taxonomy equals the production answer. The checkers-on suite run with
`KM_REFERENCE_FORCE=1` (every production route declines) publishes on every
input that P2 counts as recognised and that fits the stage's resource
budget (`KM_REFERENCE_BUDGET_S`, memory for the dense named matrix, the
ALC signature budget), and every forced answer anywhere equals the
unforced answer. The number of recognised inputs outside the budget, with
their sizes, is recorded; the release notes state the practical limit.

## P4. Nothing else changes

- C1 of v1.5.5 holds again: zero wrong answers on the held-out half and
  the 1,708-input pool under the release flags.
- S1/G8 of v1.5.5 hold again against the same-day v1.5.3 and Konclude
  binaries on Ibex: held-out mean and median within 1.10 of v1.5.3, at
  least as many verified answers as v1.5.3 and Konclude, no coverage loss
  on the pool.
- L1 and R1: the five Lean gates, the checkers-on suite, the Java tests
  and the full Rust suite pass from the tag under the certification chain
  of `.work/v155-release-cert` (adapted to the new theorem list), with
  receipts.

## P5. The boundary is stated

`lean/README.md`, `docs/CERTIFICATION-ROADMAP.md` and the release notes
say exactly: soundness is unconditional for every published answer;
completeness is proved for the EL fragment of the common clause source,
for every router decision and every fail-closed executor, up to resource
exhaustion of the reference procedure; the frontend remains outside the
boundary; HT, CB and rule completeness remain open, with the phase 2 and 3
assessments recorded.
