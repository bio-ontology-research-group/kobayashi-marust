# KM v1.6.3 plan: completeness on the hypertableau fragment

Status: draft, written 2026-11-12 during v1.6.2. Roadmap row: "hypertableau
completeness (role hierarchies, inverses, chains; an executable search
producer)".

## 1. Where v1.6.2 leaves it

The OWL capstone of v1.6.2 (`owlReference_sound`, `owlReference_complete`)
covers OWL EL with role hierarchy, chains and transitivity, and OWL ALC
without any role axiom. Inverses, symmetry, role hierarchies together with
ALC constructors, transitivity together with universal restrictions, and
role chains together with ALC constructors are outside both reference
procedures. On the checkers-on suite these are the inputs the hypertableau
routes serve.

The survey of 2026-11-09 (`docs/history/SURVEY-HT-CB-COMPLETENESS-2026-11-09.md`)
found that the HT totality theorems are conditional on a producer family
that nothing constructs, and that the crux of constructing one is a
blocking-correctness obligation (`frontierAddress`) for which no proof
exists. Making the production search executable and proving that
obligation is the expensive path.

## 2. The theorem, in the form of v1.6.0 to v1.6.2

As before, completeness comes from an exact reference procedure that runs
after the production routes decline, not from the production search:

```lean
theorem owlReference_complete (h : O.isEL ∨ O.isALC ∨ O.isSHI ∨ O.isSRI) :
    (owlReference O requested).isSome = true
```

with `owlReference_sound` unchanged in form. The new fragments:

- **ALCHI**: ALC with role inclusions, equivalent and inverse properties,
  `ObjectInverseOf` in class expressions, symmetry. Decided by type
  elimination over the closure of the TBox with a compatibility condition
  for role edges that respects the hierarchy and inverses: a type `t` may
  have an `r`-successor of type `t'` when, for every `s` with `r ⊑* s`,
  `∀s.D ∈ t` gives `D ∈ t'` and `∀s⁻.D ∈ t'` gives `D ∈ t`. ALCHI has the
  finite model property, so elimination over finite types is exact.
- **SHI**: ALCHI with transitive roles, reduced to ALCHI by the standard
  encoding: for every `∀s.C` in the closure and every transitive `t` with
  `t ⊑* s`, add `∀s.C ⊑ ∀t.∀t.C` (in clause form: the definer of `∀s.C`
  implies the definer of `∀t.∀t.C`). The reduction preserves concept
  satisfiability and subsumption between named classes (Tobies 2001,
  section 5.2); the Lean proof is the model-unravelling argument: close
  each transitive role of a model of the encoding transitively and show
  that every `∀` constraint survives.
- **SRI** (regular chains): role chains under the regularity condition of
  OWL 2, reduced to ALCHI by the automaton encoding (Horrocks, Kutz,
  Sattler 2006; Kazakov 2008): one definer per state of the role's
  automaton, propagated along edges. This is the same argument as the
  role-encoding equivalence of `docs/V1.6.2-FRONTEND-PLAN.md` section 5
  (phase B), and the two should share the closure-induction lemma.

## 3. Work items

| item | content | owner |

| ALCHI types | computable type elimination with hierarchy and inverse compatibility; exactness theorem in the style of `alcBit_exact` | lead states, agent proves |

| capstone | `owlReference` extended; `owlReference_complete` for the new fragments | lead |

| performance | type elimination is exponential; a budget as for ALC, plus a lazier elimination (only types reachable from the requested names) | agent, measured on Ibex |

The executable HT search producer and `frontierAddress` stay recorded as
the route to completeness of the production search itself; v1.6.3 does
not need them for the supervisor theorem.

## 4. Acceptance (draft)

- `owlReference_sound` and `owlReference_complete` for EL, ALC, ALCHI, SHI
  and SRI inputs, sorry-free with the standard three axioms.
- `KM_FRONTEND_CHECK` agrees with km on every suite input where both
  publish; the coverage of the suite by the new fragments is measured and
  the budget declines are listed with sizes.
- P4 of v1.6.0 holds again (C1, S1, L1/R1 on Ibex).
