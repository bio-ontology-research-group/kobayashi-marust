# KM v1.6.2 plan: the frontend in Lean

Status: plan, written 2026-11-12 during v1.6.1. Roadmap row (v1.5.6, carried
over): "Prove the frontend in Lean. A theorem must connect the clause set to
the source ontology under the OWL 2 direct semantics, covering IRI identity,
normalisation, and the regular role-inclusion encoding."

## 1. The gap

Every KM theorem up to v1.6.1 is stated on the frontend's normalised clause
stream (`List FCL`, `KMCommonRoutingSource.Entails`). The frontend
(`engine/src/frontend/`, 38,700 lines of Rust: parsing, prefix expansion,
builtin aliasing, rewrites, normalisation, role-chain and transitivity
encodings, data handling) is trusted. A wrong clause stream produces a wrong
answer that every certificate accepts.

## 2. The theorem

For an OFN document `text` inside the fragment `F` (section 3):

```lean
-- the parser is the definition of what the text means (IRI identity included)
OWL.parse text = some O
-- the Lean frontend
OWLFrontend.translate O = some T          -- T.clauses : List FCL, T.index : IRI → Option Nat
-- correctness
theorem translate_correct :
  ∀ A B ∈ O.classNames,
    OWL.EntailsSub O A B ↔ KMCommonRoutingSource.Entails T.clauses (T.index A) (T.index B)
```

with `OWL.EntailsSub` the OWL 2 direct semantics (interpretations with
nonempty domain, class and object-property extensions, the standard
constructors) for the fragment, and the analogous statement for
unsatisfiable classes and ontology inconsistency.

Two consumers:

1. **Reference path, end to end.** `km-el-reference --owl <file>` parses and
   translates in Lean, then runs the v1.6.0/v1.6.1 reference procedures on
   `T`. Capstone `kmSupervisorOWL_complete_on_EL_or_ALC`: for every OFN text
   in the OWL EL or ALC part of `F`, the supervisor publishes, and the answer
   is exact under the OWL direct semantics of the text. This needs a second
   theorem that ties the fragments: `translate` of an OWL-EL ontology is
   accepted by `recognizeEL` (and the ALC analogue), proved rather than
   measured as in v1.6.0's P2.
2. **Rust routes, checked.** `km-frontend-check <text> <rust clause stream>
   <definer map>` accepts when the Rust stream equals Lean's `T` up to the
   renaming of fresh symbols given by the definer map (both sides key fresh
   names by the structurally equal subconcept they abbreviate, as the Rust
   normaliser documents). Theorem: acceptance implies the `translate_correct`
   statement for the Rust stream, so `kmSupervisor_sound` lifts to OWL for
   every published Rust answer whose stream was checked. The check runs only
   when enabled (`KM_FRONTEND_CHECK`), like the v1.5.5 checkers, so S1 is
   unaffected; its cost is measured.

## 3. The fragment

Survey 2026-11-12 (`/mnt/data1/leechuck/km-work/v161/survey/REPORT.md`,
1,781 checkers-on inputs, all but one in functional syntax; no OWL/XML):

| fragment | all inputs | v1.6.0-recognised (592) |
|---|---:|---:|
| EL | 237 | 223 |
| EL + role hierarchy + transitivity + chains | 388 | 364 |
| ALC | 264 | 223 |
| ALC + role hierarchy + inverses | 311 | 236 |
| ALC + role hierarchy + inverses + transitivity + chains (`F`) | 499 | 389 |
| `F` + class and object-property assertions | (stage 2) | |

`F` is the choice: SubClassOf, EquivalentClasses, DisjointClasses,
ObjectPropertyDomain/Range, SubObjectPropertyOf with atomic roles and
chains, EquivalentObjectProperties, InverseObjectProperties,
ObjectInverseOf, Transitive and SymmetricObjectProperty; class expressions
over names, owl:Thing, owl:Nothing, IntersectionOf, UnionOf, ComplementOf,
SomeValuesFrom, AllValuesFrom; Declarations, annotations and prefixes. On
an `F` input the Rust passes that can fire are conformance, parse, the
bottom prepass (off the ELC route), normalisation, chain and transitivity
augmentation, domain/range clauses, inverse-bridge pruning and the ELC
demotion check; no builtin-alias rewrite fires anywhere in the suite, and
prefixed and full spellings of one IRI never co-occur, but the Lean parser
expands prefixes and compares expanded IRIs anyway. ABox assertions (147
recognised inputs are blocked by ClassAssertion alone) are stage 2, after
`F` is done; cardinalities, nominals, data, rules and the top role are out.

## 5. The proof obligation that needs design first

The role encoding is not a model-preserving rewrite. For a transitive `T`
and a negative-polarity `∀T.C`, the clauses introduce a fresh predicate
`P = __trans__T__{C}` with `P(x) ∧ T(x,y) → C(y)`, `P(x) ∧ T(x,y) → P(y)`
(and the chain analogues along the regular automaton of a role). Models of
the clauses need not interpret `T` transitively, so model restriction fails
as stated; the correct statement is preservation of class entailment.
Direction 1 (clauses entail ⇒ OWL entails): expand an OWL model by `P :=
∀T.C` (sound because the OWL model is transitive). Direction 2 (OWL entails
⇒ clauses entail): from a clause model `I`, build the OWL model whose role
extension of `T` is the transitive closure (and, for chains, the closure
under the automaton) of `I`'s, and show by induction on the class
expression, for the subformulas that occur in `F` with their polarity, that
`I ⊨ D(x)` implies the closed model satisfies `D` at `x` for positive
occurrences and the converse for negative ones; the `P` clauses are exactly
what the `∀` case needs. This is the classic elimination argument for
SH-type logics (Tobies; Horrocks and Sattler for regular RIAs); it holds for
`∀` and `∃` under `F` without number restrictions. The lead writes the
invariant before an agent starts the module.

## 6. Acceptance (draft)

- `translate_correct` and the fragment links are sorry-free with the
  standard three axioms; the capstones lift `kmSupervisor_sound` and
  `kmSupervisor_complete_on_EL_or_ALC` to OWL direct semantics.
- The Lean parser and the Rust parser agree on every `F` input of the suite
  (differential test), and `km-frontend-check` accepts the Rust stream of
  every `F` input under `KM_FRONTEND_CHECK=1`; every rejection is explained
  and either fixed in Rust or recorded as a Rust deviation.
- P4 of v1.6.0 holds again (C1, S1, L1/R1 on Ibex).
