## 2. Deliverable 1: Fresh-sample failures (`scratch/w13/fresh16.txt`)

### 2.1 `ore_ont_3708` and `ore_ont_5033`: `data_props` timeouts (300.5 s -> 6.21 s and 6.33 s)

- **Root cause**:
  - `ore_ont_3708` (55 classes, 68 object properties, 5 data properties, 16 individuals) and `ore_ont_5033` (61 classes, 70 object properties, 5 data properties, 16 individuals) are Java program ontologies containing `ObjectOneOf`, `InverseObjectProperties`, `TransitiveObjectProperty`, and `FunctionalDataProperty`, but **zero** data value-pinning constructs (`DataPropertyAssertion`, `DataHasValue`, or `DataOneOf`).
  - Previously, `KMReasoner.mayEntailNonToldDataSubProperty(snapshot)` triggered `dataPropertiesByLiteralProbes` whenever an ontology had `FunctionalDataProperty` and `ObjectOneOf`. On `3708` and `5033`, `dataPropertiesByLiteralProbes` invoked `ExtendedRun` with `--unknown-datatypes=free` over the `SHIOQ` TBox + ABox, which timed out after 15 queries x 20 s (`data_props: 296,603 ms` on `3708`, `296,658 ms` on `5033`), exhausting the 300 s ontology budget and leaving 720 subsequent queries unreached (`ok 1489/2209` and `ok 1561/2281`).
- **Fix and soundness argument**:
  - Under OWL 2 Direct Semantics, `FunctionalDataProperty(p)` combined with nominals (`ObjectOneOf`) can force two data properties $p$ and $q$ to coincide on a witness literal only when the ontology pins at least one data value via `DataPropertyAssertion`, `DataHasValue`, or `DataOneOf` (otherwise every literal interpretation is unconstrained and can assign distinct values to $p$ and $q$).
  - Tightened `KMReasoner.mayEntailNonToldDataSubProperty(snapshot)` (`R10`) to require a value-pinning construct (`DataPropertyAssertion`, `DataHasValue`, or `DataOneOf`) alongside functionality and nominals.
- **Result**:
  - `ore_ont_3708`: `data_props` drops from **296.60 s (15 timeouts)** to **0.12 s (0 timeouts)**; total wall time drops from **300.48 s (aborted)** to **6.21 s (completed, 2209 / 2209 OK)**; parity score rises from **53% to 93%**.
  - `ore_ont_5033`: `data_props` drops from **296.66 s (15 timeouts)** to **0.13 s (0 timeouts)**; total wall time drops from **300.47 s (aborted)** to **6.33 s (completed, 2281 / 2281 OK)**; parity score rises from **53% to 93%**.

### 2.2 `ore_ont_13413`: `class_expr` and `disjoint` timeouts (219.89 s with 10 timeouts -> 11.58 s with 0 timeouts)

- **Root cause**:
  - `ore_ont_13413` is an Anatomy/SPD ontology (761 classes, 5 object properties incl. `TransitiveObjectProperty`, 4 individuals) whose TBox is Horn (`ALCH+`) and whose ABox consists solely of 4 `ClassAssertion(C, a)` axioms (`hasPureClassAssertionABox(snapshot) == true`).
  - Previously, `KMReasoner.hornComplementClassAnswer(ce)` (`R17`) required `indIds.length == 0` because introducing nominals `{a}` for ABox individuals could violate convexity. As a result, `getSubClasses(ObjectComplementOf(C))`, `getInstances(ObjectComplementOf(C))`, and `getDisjointClasses(ObjectComplementOf(C))` fell back to spawning one `km classify --expr` process per query, causing 10 call timeouts (200 s) in `class_expr` and `disjoint`.
- **Fix and soundness argument**:
  - When `hasPureClassAssertionABox(snapshot)` holds (every `OWLIndividualAxiom` is `ClassAssertion(C, a)` with `C` in the Horn TBox, and there are no nominals, rules, or keys), an individual $a$ with asserted types $\{D_1, \dots, D_k\}$ has a canonical Horn model where its minimal type is $E_a = \bigsqcap_{i=1}^k D_i$. By convexity of the Horn TBox, $\mathcal{O} \models a : \neg C$ if and only if $\mathcal{T} \models E_a \sqcap C \sqsubseteq \bot$, which reduces to checking whether every named class $B \sqsubseteq C$ is disjoint from $E_a$ using the nominal-free class expression `indExpr(id)`.
  - Generalized `hornComplementClassAnswer` (`R17`) to support ontologies with `hasPureClassAssertionABox(snapshot)` via `indExpr(id)`.
- **Result**:
  - `ore_ont_13413` wall time drops from **219.89 s (10 timeouts)** to **11.58 s (0 timeouts, 2061 / 2069 OK; the remaining 8 calls are non-simple role cardinality exceptions in `class_expr`)**.

### 2.3 `ore_ont_586`: slow `entailment` and `disjoint` phases (118.57 s -> 43.04 s)

- **Root cause**:
  - `ore_ont_586` (19 classes, 20 object properties, 34 data properties, 139 individuals) contains a large ABox of 139 individuals with `DataPropertyAssertion` axioms, but zero nominals (`ObjectOneOf`, `ObjectHasValue`), zero keys (`HasKey`), zero `SameIndividual` / `DifferentIndividuals` axioms, and zero `NegativeObjectPropertyAssertion` / `NegativeDataPropertyAssertion` axioms.
  - Once `ensureHierarchy()` has verified that the full ontology (TBox + RBox + ABox) is consistent (`consistent == true`), every subsequent TBox-only query (`SubClassOf`, `DisjointClasses`, `EquivalentClasses`, and property-hierarchy entailments that do not mention individuals or nominals) spawned `ExtendedRun` over the full ontology including all 139 individuals and their data assertions.
  - Furthermore, `DataValues.candidatesForProperty` scanned all 139 declared individuals even when 100+ individuals had zero assertions in the ontology.
- **Fix and soundness argument**:
  - Added `KMReasoner.isABoxConservativeForTBox()` and `tboxQuerySource()` (`R20`): when the ontology is already known to be consistent and contains no nominals (`ObjectOneOf`, `ObjectHasValue`) in the TBox/RBox, an ABox $\mathcal{A}$ is conservative over $(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{R})$ for nominal-free TBox/RBox entailments ($\mathcal{T} \cup \mathcal{R} \cup \mathcal{A} \models \alpha \iff \mathcal{T} \cup \mathcal{R} \models \alpha$, because any model of $(\mathcal{T} \cup \mathcal{R}) \cup \{\neg\alpha\}$ can be combined via disjoint union with a model of $\mathcal{T} \cup \mathcal{R} \cup \mathcal{A}$). `canUseTBoxQuerySource(extra)` strips `ClassAssertion`, `ObjectPropertyAssertion`, `NegativeObjectPropertyAssertion`, `DataPropertyAssertion`, `NegativeDataPropertyAssertion`, `SameIndividual`, and `DifferentIndividuals` axioms from nominal-free TBox probes.
  - Added `KMReasoner.activeTargetIndividuals(snapshot)` so `DataValues.candidatesForProperty` only probes target individuals that appear in at least one logical axiom when the ontology has no universal/top-level individual forcing axioms (`SubClassOf(owl:Thing, ...)`, `ReflexiveObjectProperty`, `topObjectProperty`/`topDataProperty` domains/ranges, or `ObjectOneOf`).
- **Result**:
  - `ore_ont_586` wall time drops from **118.57 s** to **43.04 s** (0 timeouts, 100% agreement on all 42 non-bottom-property cells). Across all 98 benchmark ontologies, `R20` fires on 32 ontologies and reduces `entailment` and `disjoint` wall time by over 500 s.

### 2.4 `ore_ont_10776` and `ore_ont_16054`: 6 `isPrecomputed` unsupported calls (6 -> 0)

- **Root cause**:
  - `ore_ont_10776` and `ore_ont_16054` declare `NamedIndividual` entities in their signature (`Declaration(NamedIndividual(...))`), so `indIds.length > 0`, yet neither ontology contains any `OWLIndividualAxiom` (`ClassAssertion`, `ObjectPropertyAssertion`, etc.).
  - HermiT's `isPrecomputed(InferenceType.CLASS_ASSERTIONS)`, `OBJECT_PROPERTY_ASSERTIONS`, and `SAME_INDIVIDUAL` check whether the ontology contains any individual axioms (`m_tableau.getPermanentHyperresolutionManager()` / ABox individual axiom presence), returning `false` when there are zero `OWLIndividualAxiom`s, whereas `KMReasoner.isPrecomputed` previously checked `indIds.length > 0` and returned `false` even when `indIds.length == 0` was false, causing `ParityRun` to mark 6 `isPrecomputed` calls (`CLASS_ASSERTIONS`, `OBJECT_PROPERTY_ASSERTIONS`, `SAME_INDIVIDUAL` on `10776` and `16054`) as `km_unsupported` when HermiT returned `true` (after `precomputeInferences` was called) and KM returned `false` (or vice versa).
- **Fix**:
  - Added `KMReasoner.hasAnyIndividuals()`, which checks whether any ontology in the imports closure contains an `OWLIndividualAxiom`, and aligned `isPrecomputed` with `precomputedTypes.contains(inferenceType)` gated by `hasAnyIndividuals()` for `CLASS_ASSERTIONS` and `SAME_INDIVIDUAL`.
- **Result**:
  - All 6 `isPrecomputed` unsupported calls on `ore_ont_10776` and `ore_ont_16054` are eliminated (`isPrecomputed` is now **100% agreed (`16 / 16`)** across `fresh16.txt`).

### 3.2 Class (b) items generalized or replaced with Class (a) engine queries in W13

1. **`ForestABoxRollup` object-property assertions (`R01`)**:
   - **Before W13**: Java propagated `ObjectPropertyAssertion` edges along sub-properties, inverses, and transitive closure, and had a hardcoded `reflexiveProps.size() == 1` check that prevented `R01` from firing when multiple reflexive roles existed.
   - **After W13**: Removed the `reflexiveProps.size() == 1` restriction, quotiented ABox individuals by `SameIndividual` equivalence classes (`union-find`), and replaced Java role-value propagation with Class (a) `km` `SUB` probes (`urn:km:reflprobe`, `urn:km:reflval:<p>`, and `urn:km:ovprobe:<p>,<o>`) emitted directly in the rolled-up TBox.
2. **`KMReasoner.computeDifferentIndividuals` on rolled-up forest ABoxes (`R03`)**:
   - **Before W13**: Checked whether proxy classes `__km_ind_<a>` and `__km_ind_<b>` shared a common unsatisfiable subclass in the classified TBox (which only caught class-level conflicts).
   - **After W13**: Replaced with a Class (a) `ExtendedRun` probe testing `SubClassOf(X_b, ObjectIntersectionOf(cA, cB))` in the rolled TBox (`X_b ⊑ ⊥` in `km`).
3. **`ForestABoxRollup.tryRealizeSeparableABox` (deleted)**:
   - **Before W13**: Java-side separable-ABox realization shortcut gated by `maxInds <= 4`.
   - **After W13**: Deleted completely; `ForestABoxRollup.tryBuild` (`R01`) handles `edgeCount == 0` directly via `km` TBox classification with zero size cap.
4. **Removed arbitrary size caps in `ForestABoxRollup.tryBuildPositiveRBoxProperties` (`R05`) and `tryPregroundRules` (`R07`)**:
   - **Before W13**: `R05` had `reflexiveProps.size() == 1 && props.size() >= 100` and `!hasReflexive || !hasHasValue`; `R07` had `inds.size() > 64`.
   - **After W13**: Removed all arbitrary size caps; both reductions are now gated solely by their exact syntactic fragment preconditions and runtime engine budget/fallback.
5. **`DataValues.canUseAssertedValuesOnly` replaced with `candidatesForProperty` + batched `ExtendedRun` (`R12`)**:
   - **Before W13**: `canUseAssertedValuesOnly` skipped `km` probes when no `SubClassOf`/`EquivalentClasses` axiom mentioned `DataHasValue`.
   - **After W13**: Replaced with `DataValues.candidatesForProperty`, which harvests all candidate literals from `DataPropertyAssertion` and `DataHasValue` axioms, answers purely asserted properties directly (`R04`), and sends all remaining `(individual, property, literal)` triples in a single revision-wide `ExtendedRun` batch (`all-data-values`).
6. **`DataPropertyModel.rangesCompatible` (`R09`)**:
   - **Before W13**: Missing `ZERO_INT_SHARED` (`xsd:nonPositiveInteger` vs `xsd:nonNegativeInteger`, which share `0`), `NEGATIVE_INT_SHARED`, and `DATE_TIME_SHARED` (`xsd:dateTime` vs `xsd:dateTimeStamp`).
   - **After W13**: Added all three value-space compatibility groups and verified on 2,000 random ontologies.

### 4.3 Two real plugin bugs discovered by `RandomDifferentialTest` and fixed in W13

1. **`AsymmetricObjectProperty` falsely matched `USES_INVERSE` in `KMReasoner`**:
   - **Bug**: `KMReasoner.USES_INVERSE` included `|Asymmetric` in its regex. When an ontology had `AsymmetricObjectProperty(p)` and `SubObjectPropertyOf(r, s)` (without `InverseObjectProperties` or `ObjectInverseOf`), HermiT's `OWLAxiomsExpressivity.hasInverseRoles` was `false` (so HermiT did not link `inv(r) ⊑ inv(s)`), whereas `KMReasoner` thought the ontology used inverse roles and added `inv(r) ⊑ inv(s)`, causing `getSubObjectProperties` / `getSuperObjectProperties` on inverse properties to disagree with HermiT on seed 1 (`DISJUNCTIVE_ALCI`).
   - **Fix**: Removed `|Asymmetric` from `KMReasoner.USES_INVERSE`.
2. **`ForestABoxRollup` synthetic `ObjectInverseOf` axioms polluted `USES_INVERSE` in `KMReasoner.propertiesNow()`**:
   - **Bug**: `ForestABoxRollup.tryBuild` emits synthetic `SubClassOf(__km_ind_b, ObjectSomeValuesFrom(ObjectInverseOf(r), __km_ind_a))` axioms into `rolledSource`. `KMReasoner.propertiesNow()` checked `USES_INVERSE.matcher(querySource())` (`rolledSource`), which always matched the synthetic `ObjectInverseOf(` even when the original ontology had zero inverse property constructs (seed 3, `REFLEXIVE_FOREST_ABOX`).
   - **Fix**: Changed `KMReasoner.propertiesNow()` to match `USES_INVERSE` against `FlattenedOntology.functionalSyntaxOfSnapshot(snapshot)` (the unrolled ontology snapshot).
