# v1.5.4 routing inventory

This inventory covers every routing decision in v1.5.3 (`engine/src/routing.rs`
and its callers in `orchestrate/`). Each decision is classified under contract
G1 (`docs/V1.5.4-ACCEPTANCE.md`) as one of:

- **S**: semantic fragment or certificate;
- **C**: construct presence;
- **R**: resource decision with a single monotone threshold;
- **F**: fitted, meaning two-sided bands, conjunctions of numeric thresholds
  that choose a procedure, count equalities, or conditions read from
  inexistent clause statistics.

The last column gives the v1.5.4 action.

Admission counts on the 592 sample and on the pool come from
`km profile --explain` (step 1 of the plan). They will be filled in from the
design-half run.

## New resource rules (one threshold each, fixed on the design half)

- **R-inproc:** in-process execution below a source-size threshold (the
  existing 4 MiB).
- **R-threads:** thread count as a monotone function of logical axioms.
  Small inputs run on one thread, others on the CPU count.
- **R-memory:** bridge before CB when the predicted concurrent footprint
  exceeds the cap.
- **R-elc-parallel:** context-parallel EL completion above a class-count
  threshold.
