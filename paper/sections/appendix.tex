\section{Algorithm details}
\label{app:algorithms}

This appendix lists the optimisations that the procedures of
Section~\ref{sec:procedures} implement and the inference rules of the
consequence-based engine.  ``Default'' gives the setting with no option set in
the worker; ``auto'' marks a technique that the automatic route enables; a
named variable is the switch that enables or disables it.  ``KM'' in the origin
column marks a technique with no published counterpart that we know of.

Three further strategies are implemented but off in every route: root-ordered
resolution with a refutation read-out, splitting of residual disjunctions,
and core minimisation.  The literal ordering is partial, and the Lean
development models Hyper as resolution on an arbitrary maximal atom.
