# Review 5: Acronyms, notation, and technical terms

Reviewed manuscript SHA-256: `ac04a4bb7a96cf11c9de044aecd084d3d035540d6d4e10039c46ffabe015a15c`.

## Major findings

1. OWL 2 DL, SROIQ, EL/EL++/ELC, proof-carrying, and hypertableau needed reader-oriented introductions.
2. Consequence-based and hypertableau rule vocabulary assumed specialist knowledge.
3. Internal route labels lacked a stable mapping to fragments, workers, checkers, and fallbacks.
4. Taxonomy notation conflated the Boolean matrix, positive relation, bottom, and consistency Boolean.
5. Incremental-routing and digest terminology was not self-contained.
6. OWL profile labels and several named ontology identifiers were unexplained.

## Minor findings

1. RDF, OSGi, STAR modules, benchmark infrastructure, telemetry units, and conventional binary units needed expansion.
2. Explanation algorithms needed one-clause definitions.
3. A supplementary glossary would improve cross-disciplinary readability.

## Verdict

Core notation required repair; a compact route and acronym glossary remains advisable for the supplement.
