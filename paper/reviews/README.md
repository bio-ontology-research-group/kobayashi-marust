# Independent manuscript reviews

Seven isolated Codex subagents reviewed the same immutable manuscript snapshot, SHA-256 `ac04a4bb7a96cf11c9de044aecd084d3d035540d6d4e10039c46ffabe015a15c`. Each reviewer handled one aspect and made no edits. `review-usage.tsv` binds the aspect reports to that snapshot and their file digests. `dispositions.tsv` records the manuscript response to every numbered finding. `disposition-verification.json` is generated after revision and binds the disposition audit to the revised manuscript.

The reports are internal review evidence, not claims of external peer review. Accepted findings were integrated where the evidence supported a concrete correction. Deferred items identify submission-time work or broader extensions that cannot be added responsibly without verified sources or new experiments.
