# Review 2: Section and paragraph flow

Reviewed manuscript SHA-256: `ac04a4bb7a96cf11c9de044aecd084d3d035540d6d4e10039c46ffabe015a15c`.

## Major findings

1. The evaluation did not state which research questions the experiments and process reconstruction answer.
2. The current-biomedical corpus discussion interleaved executed OBO work with the unexecuted BioPortal protocol.
3. The transition from batch classification to incremental validation was not signposted.
4. Process evidence was introduced in reverse temporal order without first explaining its non-equivalent units.

## Minor findings

1. Requirements, routing, certification, and stateful services needed explicit hand-offs.
2. NCBitaxon, FMA, and process Methods topic changes needed cues.
3. Limitations needed an opening map of threat categories.

## Verdict

The local flow is generally sound, but several major evidence-boundary transitions required explicit wording.
