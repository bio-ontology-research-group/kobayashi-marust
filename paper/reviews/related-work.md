## Major findings

1. Moose and Baobab were absent despite being KM's direct research lineage.
2. Algorithm selection, portfolio reasoning, and pay-as-you-go OWL work were underrepresented.
3. The three calculus families needed stronger foundational positioning.
4. Proof-carrying publication needed comparison with certifying computation and skeptical checking.
5. Incremental classification and modern proof-based explanation work lacked primary citations.
6. Agentic development and biomedical ontology evaluation coverage was too narrow for the manuscript's breadth.

## Minor findings

1. OWLAPI, Protégé, Horned-OWL, and baseline-selection boundaries needed positioning.
2. The verification comparison would benefit from a dimension-by-dimension synthesis.

## Verdict

Existing citations were relevant, but the bibliography was too compact for the paper's scope.
