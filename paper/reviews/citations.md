# Review 7: Citation support and bibliographic accuracy

Reviewed manuscript SHA-256: `ac04a4bb7a96cf11c9de044aecd084d3d035540d6d4e10039c46ffabe015a15c`.

## Major findings

1. Horned-OWL did not support attributing Turtle parsing to the cited paper.
2. OpenHands was mischaracterised as primarily a bounded issue-resolution evaluation.
3. The cited Sequoia paper described an early prototype and did not support “mature.”
4. ORE, Sequoia, Kalyanpur, SWE-agent, and OpenHands bibliography records omitted verified metadata.

## Minor findings

1. The key-level citation ledger does not distinguish materially different occurrences of one key.

## Verdict

No missing keys, uncited entries, or additional unsupported occurrences were found; the listed attribution and metadata errors required correction.
