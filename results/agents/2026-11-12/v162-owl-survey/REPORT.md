# KM v1.6.2 frontend-fragment survey (report)

Scripts: survey.py (streaming 16 MB block scan, 2 processes, nice), aggregate.py. Data: per_input.jsonl, per_input.tsv (all head counts per input). Tables below are generated by aggregate.py (REPORT_tables.md).

## Summary

- Format: 1,780 of 1,781 inputs are OWL 2 functional syntax (1,094 `.owl`, 686 `.ofn`; the `.owl` ones are NOT OWL/XML). One input is Turtle (`fixtures/utf8_non_ascii.ttl`, a non-ASCII fixture, p2 status accepted on route elc; zero OFN axioms). No OWL/XML or RDF/XML input exists, so a Lean OFN parser covers the whole suite. 186 listed paths point into a removed worktree (`.work/worktrees/fix-checkers-on-suite/engine/tests/...`); the same files exist under the v1.6.1 worktree and were read from there. 13.4 GB total, 31 files above 100 MB, largest 577 MB.
- Features absent from the whole suite: HasKey, Import, DatatypeDefinition. Present but out of every candidate fragment: DLSafeRule (120 inputs), data properties and data ranges, cardinalities (ObjectMax 487, ObjectMin 404, ObjectExact 147 inputs), ObjectHasSelf (8), Reflexive/Irreflexive/Asymmetric/Functional/InverseFunctional properties, DisjointUnion (3), owl:topObjectProperty (47 inputs; 10 of them p2-accepted).
- IRI spelling: 560 inputs use prefixed names only, 1,217 full IRIs only, 4 both, 0 none. In none of the 1,781 does a prefixed name expand to an IRI that also appears as a full IRI, and none binds a non-`owl` prefix to the OWL namespace, so neither `builtin_aliases` rewrite fires anywhere in the suite. A Lean parser can therefore treat IRIs by prefix-expanded string identity, but must still expand prefixes (11 inputs declare none; the median is 5 prefixes, max 6).
- Fragment coverage (annotations and declarations ignored): F1 EL 237 of 1781 (223 of the 592 p2-accepted); F2 388 (364); F3 ALC 264 (223); F4 ALC+H+I 311 (236); F5 463 (316). Adding transitivity and chains to F4 reaches 499 (389). Of the p2-accepted inputs 364/592 are inside F2 and 389/592 inside F4+trans+chains; the largest single blockers are ClassAssertion (191 inputs, 147 of which are blocked only by it for F2), TransitiveObjectProperty (193) and ObjectPropertyChain (100).
- Non-p2 routes contribute little: 594 inputs are on the `elc` route (592 accepted, 2 not); the other routes (production_all, ht_bridge, certified_nominals, ht_rules, manual, ...) have 0 F1-F4 members except production_all (13/23/40/74), and F5 members in production_all 126, ht_bridge 15, certified_nominals 5.
- A fragment F2 (EL plus atomic role inclusions, transitivity, chains) is the best match to what v1.6.0 already recognises (364 of 592 accepted inputs, 388 total). Choosing F4+trans+chains instead raises only to 389 accepted but adds union/complement/inverse work for 110 non-accepted inputs. ABox support (F5) is the largest jump among accepted inputs (ClassAssertion appears in 191 of them, 11.5 M assertions), but the assertions dominate size, not axiom variety.

Note on the fragment definitions: F1..F5 are not nested as specified (F2 has no union/complement/forall; F4 has no transitivity or chains); x1 and x2 are extra variants. "Inside" excludes any input using owl:topObjectProperty or owl:bottomObjectProperty or Import, and treats everything outside the allowed heads (cardinalities, Functional, data, rules, Reflexive, ...) as outside.
